Put your photos in this folder.

- Name your MAIN photo:  1.jpg   (it shows at the top of the page)
- Add more as:           2.jpg, 3.jpg, 4.jpg ...
- JPG or PNG both work.

Then list them in the CONFIG "photos" array inside ../index.html.

If a photo listed in CONFIG isn't here yet, the page still works —
it just shows a small placeholder in that spot until you add it.
